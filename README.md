## About Me

I am **Celestin NTAMUTURANO**, a Rwandan Certified Public Accountant (CPA –
Full) with a Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration (Accounting,
School of Finance and Banking) and an MBA in Finance (University of Lay
Adventists of Kigali). I am a registered member of the Institute of
Certified Public Accountants of Rwanda (ICPAR), Registration No. CPA0489
(since 2018).

I bring over **13 years of experience** in Public Financial Management,
business analysis, auditing, and accounting, gained across public
institutions and private-sector consultancy assignments. My work spans
the full range of PFM business analysis functions relevant to this
assignment, including:

- Design and review of financial statements and reports
- Chart of accounts design and review
- Budgeting and accounting business process analysis
- Expenditure and revenue workflow design
- Definition of financial reporting requirements
- Functional specifications for IFMIS and related financial systems
- Internal control process design and evaluation
- Reconciliation procedures
- Other analytical and functional deliverables supporting PFM system
  implementation and improvement

I am proficient in accounting and financial management software, adapt
quickly to new working environments, and am recognized for my
diligence, analytical rigor, and ability to work effectively both
independently and within multidisciplinary teams.

The folders in this repository provide direct, verifiable evidence of
my practical contribution to each of the areas listed above.
